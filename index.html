What is the Random Seed?

The seed controls the random number generator. Same seed = same sequence of random numbers.

Why it matters: You can share a seed with others to compare strategies on the exact same market conditions. Try "always Up" vs "always Down" with the same seed to see how decisions affect outcomes.
